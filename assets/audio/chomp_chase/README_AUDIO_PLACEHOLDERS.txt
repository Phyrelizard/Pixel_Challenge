Chomp Chase placeholder audio pack

These files are intentionally simple generated WAV placeholders. Replace any of them with your own WAV/OGG/MP3 later, keeping the same filename or updating the sound key mapping in pixel_challenge_console_v28.20.5.py.

- cc_ready.wav
- cc_dot.wav
- cc_power.wav
- cc_ghost_eat.wav
- cc_player_hit.wav
- cc_fruit.wav
- cc_round_start.wav
- cc_round_clear.wav
- cc_game_over.wav
- cc_music_gameplay.wav

cc_music_gameplay.wav is also used as the temporary Chomp Chase splash music.
