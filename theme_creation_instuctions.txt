2. Walkthrough: Create a Simple Theme, Name It, Save It, Test It, View on Falcon
Here's the step-by-step workflow:

Step 1 — Open the Editor from the Console
On the console main screen, find the DMX panel on the right side
Click the "EDITOR" button in the editor button row
This calls open_dmx_editor() which overlays the full-screen DMX LIGHTING THEME EDITOR on top of the console

Step 2 — Create a New Scene
In the editor top bar, click "✚ NEW"
A dialog pops up: "Enter name for the new scene:" (defaults to "New Scene")
Type your name, e.g. "My Cool Blue" → click OK
The editor creates a blank DMXScene, saves it to dmx_scenes.json, and loads it for editing

Step 3 — Choose Your Fixtures
In Row 1 (Fixture Targets) on the left, you see a 2x8 grid of fixture canvases (F1–F16)
Click a fixture to select it (gold border = active). That fixture's current color loads into the color wheel
Use the Bank buttons (if present) to switch between fixture banks (each bank = 8 fixtures)

Step 4 — Pick Colors
On the right side of Row 1 is the Color Palette with an HSV color wheel and RGB sliders
Use the color wheel or R/G/B sliders to pick a color
The active fixture updates to that color
Click other fixtures and assign different colors, or use the Presets dropdown for pre-made palettes
The palette slots (8 swatches) let you store and recall frequently-used colors

Step 5 — Edit the Step Sequence (Scene Preview)
Row 3 shows 20 step canvases — this is the scene's animation timeline
Click any step to paint it with the current palette color
This builds your lighting sequence/pattern across the 20 steps

Step 6 — Configure Settings
Row 2 (Playback): Set pattern type, speed, fade time, direction, loop behavior
Row 4 (Settings): Trigger settings (what events activate this scene), lighting effects, saved colors
Assign buttons: Assign this scene to a console slot button for quick recall

Step 7 — Save the Scene
Click "💾 SAVE" in the top bar (or press Ctrl+S)
This writes the scene to dmx_scenes.json
The status bar shows "Saved: My Cool Blue"
Alternatively, "SAVE AS…" lets you save a copy under a new name

Step 8 — Test the Scene on the DMX Fixtures
Click "⚡ TEST SCENE" in the top bar
This calls DMXService.test_scene() which sends the scene's fixture colors as a DMX frame to the Falcon via sACN
Your physical DMX fixtures should immediately show the colors

Step 9 — Apply the Scene
Click "▶ APPLY" to apply the scene as the current active scene
This calls DMXService.apply_scene() — same as TEST but sets it as the "current scene" going forward

Step 10 — Close the Editor & Use from Console
Click "✕ CLOSE" or "◀ Back" (or press Escape)
The editor closes and on_editor_closed() fires, which:
Reloads all scenes from dmx_scenes.json into the DMXService
Refreshes the console's scene dropdown and slot button labels
Your new scene "My Cool Blue" now appears in the Scene dropdown on the console DMX panel

Step 11 — Recall from Console
Select "My Cool Blue" from the Scene dropdown → it immediately applies to all fixtures via the Falcon
Or if you assigned it to a slot button, just press that slot to instantly recall it

Step 12 — View on the Falcon
The Falcon F16V4 controller receives the sACN data at 192.168.2.113 (default IP)
Pixel universes 1–8 drive the LED pixel lanes (for game visuals)
The DMX universe (typically universe 9) drives your DMX fixtures
The _send_dmx_frame() method builds a 512-byte DMX buffer using the fixture profile's channel map (red, green, blue, strobe, dimmer, etc.) and sends it as self.falcon.sender[self.universe].dmx_data
Open the Falcon's web interface (browse to http://192.168.2.113) to see the sACN input status and verify data is arriving on the correct universe
